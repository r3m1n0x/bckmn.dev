I'm Marvin, a Senior DevOps Engineer working with Kubernetes, Linux, and platform infrastructure. My work covers production operations, troubleshooting distributed systems, and evaluating the tools and architecture behind a platform.

## What you'll find here

This is where I write down things I want to share: a useful troubleshooting detail, an experiment, a technical comparison, or a longer look at a problem. Most of it will be technical. Some of it might not be.

I'm particularly interested in how systems behave once you have to operate them: the dependencies, failure modes, upgrades, and occasional misleading error message. Kubernetes, networking, DNS, virtualization, and platform design will probably come up often.

There is room here for work in progress, including ideas that don't work out. I'll distinguish what I've tested from what I'm still exploring. A short note doesn't need to become a tutorial, and a complicated problem can take the space it needs.

## Background

My background includes training in electrical installation and IT systems integration. Today, my focus is infrastructure and operations, from Linux and virtualization to Kubernetes platforms.
