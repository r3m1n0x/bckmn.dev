I'm Marvin. Linux, Kubernetes, and virtualization are the areas I keep coming back to. I enjoy understanding how systems fit together, finding the cause of a difficult problem, and building tools that make everyday operations easier.

Today, I work as a Senior DevOps-Engineer Kubernetes. My experience spans developing and operating managed Kubernetes platforms, deploying and upgrading clusters, and investigating production issues. Linux, networking, automation, and security are all part of that work, as are supporting customers and taking part in the on-call rotation.

I work with OpenStack, Gardener, and kOps, build CI/CD pipelines, and write tools in Go, Python, and Bash to automate operational tasks. What particularly interests me is how systems behave once you have to operate them: their dependencies, failure modes, upgrades, and the occasional misleading error message.

## How I work

I'm a generalist who enjoys getting into the details: following a failure across dependencies, understanding an unfamiliar system, or writing a small tool that makes an operational task easier. I work closely with colleagues and customers, and share what I learn through documentation and workshops.

## Earlier in my career

I started in electrical and data network installation, with hands-on installation work, site coordination, and team leadership. At DaNet, that included supervising technicians, coordinating materials, and working directly with customers across industrial, educational, and private projects.

At FoxJobsAG, a major part of my work involved **nuclear power plant construction, repair, and deconstruction projects in Switzerland, France, and Italy**, primarily for General Electric. I worked as a site manager and team leader on electrical installations and IT systems. This is a chapter of my life that I'm proud of, both for the practical work and the responsibility I took on.

I later retrained as an IT specialist in systems integration at Triology, completing the programme in January 2020. From there, I moved into Linux, container infrastructure, and Kubernetes operations, working on the Cloudogu Ecosystem and delivering Docker and Kubernetes workshops for colleagues and customers. That path brought me to the platform engineering and operations work I do today.

My [CV](/cv/) has the full work history, technical skills, and qualifications.

## What I'm building

I'm currently working on **Propagator**, a Kubernetes operator written in Go for managing services across multiple clusters. The aim is to make service deployment and lifecycle management declarative, using Kubernetes custom resources and Helm, with Gardener as the initial target environment.

It's an ongoing development project. You can find a short [project overview in my CV](/cv/#projects).
